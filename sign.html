Sign and label maker

Design a 3D-printable sign with visual lettering and braille together. Defaults follow the dimensions in ADA 703.2 and 703.3 (California-pinned braille spacing) — the compliance notes below tell you what a single-material print can and cannot satisfy. Nothing you type leaves this page.

The sign maker needs JavaScript. Lettering geometry and the 3D preview are computed entirely in your browser; there is no server to fall back to.

Each line becomes a line of lettering. Type lowercase and capitalize only proper names, initials and acronyms — sign braille uses capital indicators only for those, and the braille follows what you type. The visual lettering is displayed uppercase for tactile reading either way.

ADA 703.3.1 requires contracted braille on signs.

Leave empty to translate the sign text. Fill to supply exact cells instead — one character per cell, as in BRF.

Sign summary

    These defaults follow the dimensions in ADA 703.2/703.3.1 and California 11B-703. A single-material print cannot satisfy the visual-contrast and non-glare finish requirements — print the flush two-piece style in contrasting colors, or pair a tactile-only sign with a separate visual sign. Mounting: tactile characters between 48" and 60" above the floor, latch side of the door. This tool does not provide legal or code advice.

    Lettering

    All bundled faces are open-licensed. Regular weights meet the ADA raised-character stroke rule; bold reads better recessed or flush.

    Height of a capital letter. ADA tactile window: 15.9–50.8 mm (5/8"–2").

    Flush exports two files: the plate with recesses and the letter inserts, printed in a second color and glued in level.

    Height above (or depth into) the plate. ADA raised minimum: 0.8 mm (1/32").

    Plate

    Space between content and the plate edge. The plate sizes itself around the content — braille spacing never shrinks.

    ADA 703.3.2 minimum: 9.5 mm (3/8"); California caps it at 12.7 mm (1/2").

    Mounting holes

    Clearance fit — the screw passes through freely.

    Set automatically from the fastener; edit freely.
