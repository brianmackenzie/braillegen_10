Braille asset generator

Type text, verify the braille, and export it as a 3D-printable STL tile, a physically-accurate SVG for laser cutting or swell paper, an embosser-ready BRF file, or plain Unicode braille. Translation runs liblouis in your browser — nothing you type leaves this page.

Each line becomes its own braille line. The preview updates as you type.
